From signed to live

For 5,869 of the 6,732 wins awarded from August 2022 to September 2025 with a contract start on record: the median start came 0 days after the award. 62.4% started on the award day, 15.3% before it, which means the paperwork followed the work, and 5.1% started 30 or more days after. For 3,723 wins with an expected start on the tender, 56.2% started within a week of it, 22.0% 30 or more days late and 3.3% 30 or more days early. The award itself was published a median 12 days after it was made, and 22.5% came 90 or more days after.

Amounts

Only 41.3% of wins carry a usable amount in Canadian dollars; the rest are blank, zero or one dollar. That is why every forecast here counts wins, not dollars. Among wins with an amount, the median was CA$194,638 and the middle half ran from CA$73,778 to CA$724,472.
